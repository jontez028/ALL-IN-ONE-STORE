Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information ("we", "us") collects when you use our website and our shop, why we collect it, who sees it, and what rights you have. We follow the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

1. Who is responsible for your data

is the data controller. Our address is . You can reach us on or at .

2. What we collect

  • Your account: your name, phone number, email address and password. We never see your password. Our login service keeps it in scrambled form.
  • Delivery details: your county and your delivery address, such as town, estate and landmark.
  • Your orders: the items you buy, the amounts, the delivery fee, the status and the time of each step, and the payment method. For M-Pesa we also keep the confirmation code and the phone number used. We never see your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Shop purchases: the items, the amount, the payment method, the M-Pesa code, the date, and the name of the worker who served you. We do not need your name for a shop sale.
  • Messages: what you send us on WhatsApp, by phone, or in a quote request.
  • On your device: the store keeps your cart, your sign-in and your recent choices in your browser so the store works. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers.

3. Why we use it

  • To take, pack, deliver and track your orders.
  • To confirm payments and to keep accounting and tax records.
  • To contact you about an order or a problem with it.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns and warranty claims.
  • To keep the store secure and to prevent fraud.
  • To meet our legal duties.

We send marketing messages only if you agree, and you can stop them at any time.

4. Who we share it with

  • Delivery riders and couriers: your name, phone number, address and what is being delivered, only to deliver your order.
  • Safaricom M-Pesa: to take and confirm your payment.
  • Supabase: the online service that stores our database, including accounts and orders.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files. It sees normal visit information such as your IP address. It does not receive your account data.
  • WhatsApp: if you choose to contact us there.
  • Authorities: when the law requires us to.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Where your data is stored

Our database is hosted by Supabase in Mumbai, India, so your data is stored outside Kenya. We use it only to run the store. The provider protects it with encryption and access controls, as the Data Protection Act requires for transfers outside Kenya.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account details while your account is open. We keep order and sales records for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise everything the law does not require us to keep.

7. How we protect it

The store uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only the owner and authorised workers can see orders, and workers see only what they need for their job. The database rules limit each customer to their own information. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and do not share it.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used;
  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or out of date;
  • object to some uses of your data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • withdraw your consent to marketing at any time.

To use any of these rights, call or write to us using the details above. We may need to confirm who you are first. We will reply as soon as possible and within the time the Act requires. You can change your delivery details yourself on your account page.

9. Children

The store is for adults aged 18 and over. If you are younger, please ask a parent or guardian to place orders for you.

10. Complaints

Please tell us first so we can put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya at www.odpc.go.ke.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
