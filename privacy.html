Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version

  • We keep only what we need to sell to you and deliver your order.
  • We do not sell your details, and we do not use advertising trackers.
  • Your information is stored securely and only the people who need it can see it.
  • You can ask to see it, fix it or delete it at any time.

Who we are

("we") runs this website and the shop. We decide how your information is used, so we are the "data controller" under the Data Protection Act, 2019. You can find us at , call , or write to .

What we collect

When you create an account, we ask for your name, phone number, email and a password. We never see your password in plain form, and we never see your M-Pesa PIN.

When you order, we keep your county and delivery address, what you bought, what you paid, how you paid and where the order has got to. For M-Pesa that includes the confirmation code and the phone number you paid from.

When you buy in the shop, we keep the items, the amount, the payment method and the name of the worker who served you. We do not ask for your name.

If you message us on WhatsApp, by phone or through a quote request, we keep what you send so we can reply.

Your phone or computer also stores your cart and your sign-in so the store works between visits. We do not use advertising cookies.

How we use it

We use your details to take and deliver your order, confirm your payment, keep the accounting and tax records the law asks for, answer your questions, handle returns and warranty claims, and protect the store from fraud. We will send you marketing messages only if you say yes, and you can say stop whenever you like.

Who else sees it

Delivery riders get your name, phone number, address and the items, and nothing more. Safaricom handles your M-Pesa payment. If you contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp sees that conversation. We may also have to share information with the police, the tax authority or a court when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Where it is kept and how we protect it

Your information is stored securely. It is protected by encryption and by access rules that let each customer see only their own account, and let workers see only what they need for their job. Our servers may be outside Kenya. Where that happens, we make sure the information stays protected as the Data Protection Act requires.

No system is perfectly safe, so please use a strong password and keep it to yourself.

How long we keep it

We keep your account for as long as it is open. Order and sales records stay for as long as tax and accounting law says we must. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise everything we are not required to keep.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy, correct anything that is wrong, stop using your information for a particular purpose, or delete it. You can also take back your consent to marketing at any time.

To do any of this, call or write to us using the details above. We may ask you to prove who you are first, so we do not hand your information to someone else. We will answer within the time the law allows. You can change your delivery details yourself on your account page.

Children

The store is for people aged 18 and over. If you are younger, ask a parent or guardian to order for you.

Complaints

If something is not right, tell us first and we will try to fix it. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at www.odpc.go.ke.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will put the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
