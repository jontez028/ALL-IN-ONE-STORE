Your legal rights

Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012, goods must match their description and work as they should. Nothing on this page limits those rights.

Faulty, damaged or wrong items

Tell us within days of delivery or purchase. Call or message with your order number, a short description, and clear photos or a video. Please do not use or repair the item before we have seen it.

If the item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, we will replace it or refund you, whichever you prefer, and we pay for the return delivery.

If you changed your mind

You can return an unopened item in its original packaging within days of delivery or purchase. You pay for the return delivery. Clothes and shoes must be unworn, unwashed and still have their tags.

What we cannot take back

We cannot take back phones, laptops, tablets or earbuds that have been opened and used, unless they are faulty. We also cannot accept items damaged by misuse, water, drops or repairs by someone else, items missing their original accessories or packaging, or underwear and other sealed hygiene items once the seal is broken.

Warranty

Many products come with a warranty from the maker or the seller. The length is on the product or on the warranty card in the box. Keep your receipt or order number. If a warranty item fails, contact us and we will help with the claim or the repair.

How a return works

  1. Contact us with your order number, or bring your receipt to the shop.
  2. We tell you whether the return is accepted and how to send or bring the item.
  3. We check the item when it arrives.
  4. We replace it or send your refund.

Refunds

Refunds go back the way you paid. For M-Pesa, that is the phone number you paid from. We pay approved refunds within working days. If you paid cash on delivery, we refund by M-Pesa to a number you give us.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel before the order is dispatched, and we will refund anything you paid. Once it is out for delivery, refuse it at the door or contact us, and the return rules above apply.

Shop purchases

If you bought in the shop, bring your receipt and the item. The same rules apply.
