Returns Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

We want you to be happy with what you buy. This page explains what to do if something is wrong, and when you can return an item or get a refund.

Your legal rights stay. Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012, goods must match their description and work as they should. Nothing on this page limits those rights.

1. Faulty, damaged or wrong items

  • Tell us within days of delivery or purchase. Call or message us on with your order number, a short description, and clear photos or a video.
  • Do not use or repair the item before we have looked at it.
  • If the item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, we will replace it or refund you, whichever you prefer. We pay for the return delivery.

2. Changed your mind

  • You can return an unopened item in its original packaging within days of delivery or purchase. You pay the return delivery.
  • Clothes and shoes must be unworn, unwashed and still have their tags.

3. What we cannot take back

  • Phones, laptops, tablets and earbuds that have been opened and used, unless they are faulty.
  • Items damaged by misuse, water, drops or repairs by someone else.
  • Underwear and other items sold sealed for hygiene reasons once the seal is broken.
  • Items without their original accessories or packaging.

4. Warranty

Many products come with a maker's or seller's warranty. The length is shown on the product or on the warranty card in the box. Keep your receipt or order number. If a warranty item fails, contact us and we will help you with the claim or the repair.

5. How a return works

  1. Contact us with your order number, or bring your receipt to the shop.
  2. We tell you whether the item is accepted and how to send or bring it back.
  3. We check the item when we receive it.
  4. We replace the item or send your refund.

6. Refunds

  • Refunds go back to the way you paid. For M-Pesa that means to the phone number you paid with.
  • We pay approved refunds within working days.
  • For cash on delivery, we refund by M-Pesa to a number you give us.

7. Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order before it is dispatched, and we will refund anything you paid. Once it is out for delivery, please refuse it at the door or contact us, and the returns rules above apply.

8. Shop purchases

If you bought in the shop, bring your receipt and the item. The same rules apply.
